Offset a standardized amount of your business’s carbon footprint each month based on employee count.

Most office or retail businesses generate up to 0.5 mT of carbon emissions per employee every month from energy use, waste, travel, shipping, and commuting. This plan multiplies that estimate by your employee count and automatically purchases verified carbon credits.

Credits come from a global portfolio of projects that restore natural climate resilience, protect ecosystems, and reduce climate super-pollutants. You’ll receive a certificate of purchase and can adjust your employee count or cancel anytime.

*0.5 metric ton CO₂e per employee per month is a standardized allocation used for this subscription. Actual emissions vary by business activity, location, energy use, commuting, travel, shipping, and waste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do carbon offsets work?

Carbon offsets neutralize your footprint by funding projects like reforestation and landfill gas capture, which remove or prevent greenhouse gas emissions. Each credit represents a verified reduction or removal of emissions equivalent to a specific amount of CO₂e.

What are the benefits of buying carbon offsets?

Offsets allow your business to be accountable for unavoidable emissions while you work on conservation and efficiency. They demonstrate leadership, align with customer values, and help engage employees in climate action.

How do you ensure projects really make a difference?

Projects are independently verified under recognized standards and must pass “additionality” tests, meaning your purchase is critical to making the reductions happen beyond business-as-usual scenarios.
